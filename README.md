# AI-Assisted CV Homepage

A personal homepage that doubles as an intelligent introduction to you. Visitors can chat with an AI assistant that knows your background — gleaned from your CV, public profile content, and any private context you choose to share only with the AI.

## What It Does

- **Personal showcase** — Upload your CV and enrich your homepage with descriptions, honors, projects, and other highlights you want the world to see.
- **AI assistant** — An embedded conversational AI lets visitors ask questions and get thoughtful, personalized answers about your background, skills, and experience.
- **Public + private knowledge** — The AI draws from two sources: the public content displayed on your homepage, and a private notes section visible only to the AI. This lets you give the assistant extra context (interview availability, salary expectations, personal anecdotes) without exposing it directly on the page.

## How It Works

1. **Owner setup** — You provide your CV, write public-facing content, and optionally add private notes for the AI only.
2. **Visitor experience** — Visitors browse your homepage and can open a chat window to ask the AI anything about you.
3. **AI synthesis** — The assistant combines public homepage content with your private notes to generate accurate, contextually aware responses.

## Use Cases

- Stand out during job searches by giving recruiters an interactive way to learn about you.
- Let collaborators or conference attendees get quick answers about your work.
- Replace the static "About Me" page with a dynamic, conversational experience.
